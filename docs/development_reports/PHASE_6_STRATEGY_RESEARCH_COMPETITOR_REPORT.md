1. **Strategy Agent (`StrategyAgent`)**: Generates market positioning, value propositions, elevator pitches, messaging pillars, channel selection, and strictly validates that funnel budget allocation percentages sum to **100.0%**.

3. **Competitor Agent (`CompetitorAgent`)**: Evaluates identified competitors, conducts SWOT analysis, benchmarks pricing strategies, maps 2D positioning coordinates, and formulates high-impact counter-messaging differentiators.

Each agent strictly conforms to the **Phase 5 Agent Contract and Responsibility System** and introduces **explicit Data Provenance separation** (`observed_data`, `model_prediction`, `llm_inference`, `recommendation`) to guarantee transparency and eliminate external data hallucinations.

### 2.1 Data Provenance Hierarchy (`DataProvenance`)

To enforce scientific integrity and eliminate hallucination:
- **`observed_data`**: Verbatim customer parameters extracted from `CampaignContext.brief` (budget, target market, product specifications, declared competitors).
- **`model_prediction`**: ML regression or classification inferences (e.g. Strategy propensity score $= 1$, Topic models from `research/models/research/`).
- **`llm_inference`**: Strategic qualitative synthesis generated by LLMs (e.g. positioning statements, messaging angles, persona pain points).
- **`recommendation`**: Prescriptive downstream operational guidance (e.g. budget reallocations, bid tactics, differentiators).

## 3. Detailed Agent Implementation

### 3.1 Strategy Agent (`src/adpilot/agents/strategy_agent.py`)
- **Base Class:** `BaseAgent[StrategyAgentInput, StrategyAgentOutput]`
- **Contract:** Registered under `STRATEGY_AGENT_CONTRACT`
- **RAG & ML Integration:** Retrieves contextual market documents via `RAGService` and executes ML strategy propensity inference.
- **Funnel Constraint Enforcement:** Validates that `sum(f.budget_allocation_percent for f in funnel_strategy) == 100.0`.
- **Fault-Tolerant Deterministic Fallback:** Automatically activates if LLM endpoints or external networks are unavailable.
- **Lifecycle Telemetry:** Emits structured `agent_started`, `agent_completed`, and `agent_failed` events to `event_bus`.

### 3.2 Research Agent (`src/adpilot/agents/research_agent.py`)
- **Base Class:** `BaseAgent[ResearchAgentInput, ResearchAgentOutput]`
- **Contract:** Registered under `RESEARCH_AGENT_CONTRACT`
- **Context Awareness:** Ingests `context.brief` and upstream `context.strategy` outputs to ensure aligned persona definitions and keyword research.
- **Synthesized Fields:**
  - `audience_personas`: Multi-tiered personas with pain points, goals, and interests.
  - `channel_benchmarks`: CPC and CTR baselines per distribution channel.
  - `recommended_keywords`: Target SEO/SEM keyword strings.
  - `trending_topics`: Real-time market trends scored by relevance.
- **Lifecycle Telemetry:** Emits events with model metadata, latency, and confidence scores.

### 3.3 Competitor Agent (`src/adpilot/agents/competitor_agent.py`)
- **Base Class:** `BaseAgent[CompetitorAgentInput, CompetitorLandscape]`
- **Contract:** Registered under `COMPETITOR_AGENT_CONTRACT`
- **Context Awareness:** Ingests upstream `context.strategy` and `context.research` outputs.
- **Synthesized Fields:**
  - `competitors`: Detailed SWOT matrices and messaging analysis.
  - `pricing_comparison_summary`: Pricing model vulnerability analysis.
  - `positioning_map`: Coordinate placement across market axes.
  - `differentiators`: Explicit competitive advantages against market incumbents.
- **Lifecycle Telemetry:** Emits events with model metadata, latency, and confidence scores.

### 3.4 Orchestrator Integration (`src/adpilot/orchestrator/master_orchestrator.py`)
- `MasterOrchestrator` maintains direct instances of `StrategyAgent`, `ResearchAgent`, and `CompetitorAgent`.
- `_dispatch_agent()` seamlessly routes planned steps to the concrete agents and accumulates structured outputs onto `CampaignContext`.
